## 1. Price Trend & Market Intelligence (`models/price_predictor.py`)

- **Objective**: Forecast 15-30 day ahead modal prices for MSP crops across UP mandis to help farmers decide whether to sell at MSP or open market.
- **Data Ingestion**:
  - Historical Agmarknet Mandi arrival volumes (Quintals) and modal prices (₹/Q).
  - CACP Kharif/Rabi Minimum Support Price official notifications.
  - Rainfall departure indices (IMD gridded data) & festive demand spikes.
- **Model Architecture**: Gradient Boosted Trees (LightGBM) with lag features (t-1, t-7, t-30), rolling moving averages (7d, 14d, 30d), and seasonal Fourier terms.
- **Confidence Metrics**: Predicts upper/lower bounds with 90% confidence intervals.

## 2. Smart Centre Recommendation Engine (`src/lib/demoData.ts` & Backend `/ai/recommend-centres`)

- **Objective**: Automatically match farmers to optimal procurement centres to prevent crowding and minimize travel.
- **Scoring Function**:
  $$\text{Score} = w_1 \cdot \text{CropSupport} + w_2 \cdot (1 - \frac{\text{Distance}}{D_{\max}}) + w_3 \cdot \frac{\text{CapacityRemaining}}{\text{TotalCapacity}} + w_4 \cdot \text{Facilities}$$
- **Weights**:
  - Distance: 35%
  - Real-time center queue load: 30%
  - Supported crop eligibility & quality lab: 25%
  - Electronic weighbridge & logistics access: 10%

---

## 3. Dynamic Queue & Waiting Time Estimator (`models/queue_optimizer.py`)

- **Objective**: Prevent multi-hour traffic jams at mandis during harvest peaks.
- **Model**: Extended $M_t / G / c$ queueing system with time-varying arrival rates $\lambda(t)$ and service times $\mu$ conditioned on moisture testing, bag unloading, and electronic weighment.
- **Dynamic Slot Re-balancing**: If a procurement center experiences bottlenecks (e.g. moisture tester delay), pending tokens receive proactive SMS notifications advising 45-minute delayed arrival.

---

## 4. Grounded Bilingual RAG Assistant (`models/rag_assistant.py`)

- **Objective**: Provide conversational query resolution in Hindi and English without hallucinations.
- **Zero Hallucination Guarantee**: Every answer generated by Kisan Sahayak links to verified data sources (CACP Gazette, Agmarknet API feed, or State Civil Supplies notifications) with timestamps.
- **Architecture**: Vector embeddings (bge-m3 / multilingual-e5) stored in PostgreSQL pgvector, retrieved via cosine similarity with strict threshold filtering.
